# 0005 · Logo

Status: accepted, 2026-11-11 (Nik, after several rounds).

## Decision

- **Mark: the skill radar.** A hexagon with spokes and two inner grid rings (one axis per track), your strengths filled in ember with a dot on every axis. It stands for the whole platform (many tracks, a skill profile), not only git. Source: `docs/brand/radar.svg`; in the app `app/components/AppLogo.vue`.
- **Wordmark: Tektur**, lowercase, "grind" in weight 900 and "stone" in weight 500 in ember (`--ui-primary`). Font token `--font-brand`, loaded through `nuxt.config.ts` (`fonts.families`).
- **Small sizes:** the 16 px favicon and `favicon.svg` use a simpler cut (no inner rings, no dots), because the full mark blurs at that size.
- **Files:** `public/favicon.svg` (light/dark aware), `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `og-image.png` (share image, 1200×630). The raster files are generated by `node scripts/brand/generate-icons.mjs`; change the script, not the PNGs.
- **Kept, not used yet:** the wheel mark (a grindstone wheel with one freshly ground, lit edge), `docs/brand/wheel.svg`. Nik wants to keep it.

## Why

Earlier directions (a wheel grinding `</>`, pixel art, terminal prompts, gems) were rejected as unclear or too busy. The radar was the one mark that says what the platform is about; the grid rings and value dots make it read as a chart. Tektur gives a game-like feel through the letterforms alone, and the ember "stone" ties the name to the ember in the mark.
